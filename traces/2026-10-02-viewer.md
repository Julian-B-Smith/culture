# viewer — dev visualizer for the canonical core

- **Queue item:** unqueued: the human asked for a visualizer viewable through a dev server. Explicitly not ROADMAP P2, whose render stack is still the human's decision.
- **Why:** watch the canonical C++ core run, and judge P4's changed dynamics by eye (PR #8).
- **Evidence consulted:** reference/ui.js (palette, colours by birth order); core.hpp (World, Event); ROADMAP P2 (to stay out of its decision).
- **What changed:**
  - `viewer/` (`stream.cpp`, `server.js`, `index.html`, README);
  - CMake target `culture_stream`;
  - `.claude/launch.json` (`viewer`, port 5180);
  - README and CODEMAP pointers.
  - No new dependencies: Node built-ins, C++ standard library.
- **Checked in the browser pane:**
  - 30 fps at 220×140 and 16 gens/frame;
  - surprise, mutation and spark births appear with family colours, ledger and rings;
  - no console errors.
  - Pause was first wrong: the core ran on into OS buffers and dumped ~1,500 gens on resume. Fixed with SIGSTOP/SIGCONT; re-measured: paused gen held at 1,440, and resume advanced 976 gens in 2 s against 960 expected.
- **Follow-up from the human's first look (2026-10-02):**
  - Full screen was missing; added (button, `F`/`Esc`, fading HUD).
  - At 550×350 the world sat at 1× because zoom was integer-only; it now fills the space at fractional zoom.
  - Full screen was proven in the pane: it neither entered nor rejected there. Checking the effective state after 400 ms, rather than trusting the promise, engages the in-page fallback; exit restores the controls.
  - Tiers moved into their own scroll box, with events in another, and the controls fixed. Measured at 1280×800, stress profile, 11 tiers: the ledger scrolls, the panel does not, Restart stays at y = 286 after scrolling the ledger to the bottom, and the scroll position survives the 4 Hz ledger refresh.
  - PR #9's conflict with #8 (both appended CMake targets) was resolved by merging main, not rebasing (no force-push).
- **Alternatives rejected:** a WebAssembly build (emscripten not installed; it is in the backlog per D-030); WebSockets (would need a dependency or a hand-rolled protocol, where a streamed HTTP response suffices).
- **Verify:** `./verify fast` exit 0 before commit.
- **Open questions:** none.
